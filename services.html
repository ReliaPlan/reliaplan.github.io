Services

ReliaPlan provides practical, data‑driven support to improve equipment reliability, optimize maintenance programs, and strengthen asset performance. Our focus is on solutions that work in real operating environments — simple, effective, and sustainable.

Reliability & Maintenance Program Development

We help organizations build or improve reliability and maintenance programs that reduce unplanned downtime and increase asset availability. This includes program structure, roles, workflows, and performance expectations.

Preventive & Predictive Maintenance Planning

Development and optimization of PM and PdM strategies, including task development, interval setting, and alignment with asset criticality and failure modes.

Asset Criticality Analysis

Structured evaluation of equipment to determine criticality rankings, maintenance priorities, and risk‑based resource allocation.

Failure Mode & Effects Analysis (FMEA)

Facilitated FMEA sessions to identify failure modes, consequences, and mitigation strategies that improve reliability and reduce operational risk.

CMMS Optimization & Workflow Design

Improving work order processes, maintenance workflows, and CMMS configuration to ensure accurate data, efficient execution, and meaningful reporting.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Structured RCA support to identify true causes of failures and implement corrective actions that prevent recurrence.

Maintenance KPIs & Performance Tracking

Development of practical, meaningful KPIs such as MTBF, MTTR, PM compliance, backlog health, and schedule adherence — with dashboards and reporting structures that drive action.

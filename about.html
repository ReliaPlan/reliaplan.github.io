About

ReliaPlan is a veteran-owned firm specializing in reliability, maintenance, and mechanical integrity solutions for industrial facilities. With more than 30 years of experience supporting petrochemical plants, manufacturing operations, and heavy industries, ReliaPlan provides practical, field-tested technical support that improves efficiency, minimizes downtime, and ensures regulatory compliance.

Our expertise spans the full lifecycle of equipment and asset management, including maintenance planning and scheduling, reliability programs, mechanical integrity assessments, and turnaround support. We deliver solutions that are technically sound, operationally realistic, and aligned with industry best practices.

Maintenance Management

  • Maintenance planning and scheduling
  • Optimization of maintenance processes and workflows
  • Program assessments and gap analysis
  • Turnaround planning and execution support

Reliability Systems

  • Equipment criticality analysis
  • Predictive and preventative maintenance strategy development
  • Spare parts strategy and BOM development
  • Equipment health monitoring and performance evaluation

Mechanical Integrity

  • Mechanical integrity program development
  • API compliance support (API 510, 570, 653)
  • Repair recommendations and technical evaluations

ReliaPlan is committed to delivering high-quality engineering support with a focus on safety, reliability, and operational excellence. Our clients rely on us as a trusted partner who brings deep expertise, disciplined execution, and practical solutions to every project.

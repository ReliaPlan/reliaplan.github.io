About

ReliaPlan is a veteran‑owned consulting firm providing reliability, maintenance, and mechanical integrity support to industrial facilities. We help organizations strengthen asset performance, reduce downtime, and maintain compliance in demanding operational environments.

Maintenance Management

A strong maintenance foundation improves efficiency, reduces cost, and supports long‑term equipment health. ReliaPlan helps organizations enhance their preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance programs.

  • Maintenance planning & scheduling
  • Work order process optimization
  • Maintenance excellence assessments
  • Turnaround scope development & job planning support

Reliability Engineering

Improving reliability is essential for uptime and risk reduction. We apply practical reliability engineering methods to strengthen asset performance and prevent failures.

  • Equipment criticality analysis
  • Predictive maintenance & spare parts strategies
  • Equipment health monitoring assessments & recommendations

Mechanical Integrity

Protecting fixed equipment is critical for safety, compliance, and operational continuity. ReliaPlan provides evaluations and program support to help facilities maintain integrity and meet industry standards.

  • Mechanical Integrity (MI) program assessments & support
  • Pressure vessel & piping integrity evaluations
  • API 510 / 570 / 653 compliance support

Committed to Excellence

ReliaPlan is known for practical solutions, technical expertise, and a commitment to operational excellence. Our mission is to help organizations build reliable, efficient, and compliant operations across petrochemical, manufacturing, and heavy industrial environments.

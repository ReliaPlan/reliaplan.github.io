About

ReliaPlan is a veteran-owned firm specializing in reliability and maintenance solutions that help industries maximize asset performance, minimize downtime, and ensure compliance. With over 30 years of experience, we provide expert guidance to petrochemical facilities and heavy industries, delivering sustainable results that improve efficiency and protect critical assets.

Maintenance Management

A strong maintenance system improves efficiency and reduces costs. We help organizations strengthen their preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance strategies for long-term success.

  • Maintenance planning & scheduling
  • Work order process optimization
  • Maintenance excellence assessments
  • Turnaround scope development & job planning support

Reliability - Preventing failures is key to long-term asset health. Our advanced reliability practices improve uptime and reduce risks.

  • Root cause analysis (RCA)
  • Failure mode & effects analysis (FMEA)
  • Asset criticality ranking
  • Reliability program development
